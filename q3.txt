1. mTransform is the best candidate--it's non-specific to sprites, as it's a scale and position function that can be easily applied
to other objects with useful purpose. Easy examples include colliders, cameras, and even sound triggers.

2. Render sprites that are not translucent first--prioritizng background elements, then do the translucent sprites to avoid layering issues and 
sprites muddling each other. Between translucent sprites, back to front is preferable because blending isn't commutative--order does matter in this case.
